# ottimo

### What 

Ottimo is a collection of web development and consultancy services, that are centered around web performance optimisation, aimed at small to medium business whether they have a website or plan to launch one. It is founded and run by @abadiu, a Southampton based web developer with more than 4 years experience developing big and small websites and apps. Check it out, it's live at [ottimo.site](https://ottimo.site).

### Why

How your site performs when a customer uses it, determines how effective your site really is at converting visitors into customers. Ideally any business that does even a small bit of business online should be concerned with how their customers use their site. How fast does your website load, does it take longer than 5 seconds? According to studies the average internet user expects a webpage to load in under 2 seconds, so if you're site is under that threshold your website is much more likely to experience dropoffs from vistors instead of turning them into customers. Ottimo's mission is to work closely with our clients in achieving a website that is not only fast to load and use, but also user friendly and, realiable on any device.
